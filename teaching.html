AGEC 115 — Decision Tools for Agricultural Economics and Agribusiness

Instructor, Fall 2026

AGEC 115 introduces students to spreadsheet-based tools used to organize data, evaluate alternatives, and support decision-making in agricultural economics and agribusiness.

  • Translating economic problems into structured decision tools
  • Building and interpreting Excel formulas and logical functions
  • Data visualization and communication
  • Hands-on problem solving
  • Clear interpretation of quantitative results

Teaching philosophy

I aim to make quantitative tools approachable by connecting technical concepts to realistic decisions. Rather than treating software as an end in itself, I encourage students to understand the economic logic behind each calculation and communicate results clearly.
