About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Agricultural Economics at Kansas State University. My research focuses on production economics, risk and uncertainty, and farm finance, with applications to crop insurance, agricultural policy, agricultural investment, and climate-related production risk.

I grew up in Nepal in a farming family, an experience that shaped my interest in how agricultural producers make decisions when outcomes are uncertain. Before beginning my doctoral studies, I earned an M.S. in Agricultural Economics from the University of Arkansas and a B.S. in Agriculture from Agriculture and Forestry University, Nepal.

Research interests

  • Production economics
  • Economics of risk and uncertainty
  • Farm finance
  • Crop insurance and agricultural policy
  • Climate risk and agricultural adaptation
  • Applied econometrics and statistical learning

Methods & software

Panel-data econometrics, causal inference, dynamic structural modeling, generalized method of moments, stochastic simulation, risk-return analysis, forecasting, machine learning, and data visualization.

R · Python · Stata · SQL · Power BI · Microsoft Excel
