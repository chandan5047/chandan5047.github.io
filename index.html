Chandan Bhattarai

Ph.D. Candidate in Agricultural Economics · Kansas State University

My research lies at the intersection of production economics, risk and uncertainty, and farm finance, with applications to crop insurance, agricultural policy, climate risk, and farm investment decisions.

I study the risks agricultural producers face, their preferences toward those risks, and the incentives created by markets and government programs. My work combines economic theory, econometric methods, simulation, and statistical learning to understand producer behavior and inform agricultural decision-making.
