Research

My research examines how agricultural producers respond to risk and how risk-management strategies, markets, and public policy affect production and investment decisions.

Estimating producer risk preferences

This project estimates producer risk preferences using a dynamic model of production, consumption, and investment decisions. The empirical analysis uses a large farm-level panel and a two-step generalized method of moments framework.

Previous crops, wheat yield, and production risk

This research examines how previous crops affect winter wheat yield and yield risk across Kansas and Oklahoma variety trials, including downside risk, weather exposure, and heterogeneity across locations and varieties.

Crop insurance, contracting, and moral hazard

This project studies the interaction between crop insurance and forward contracting in an expected-utility framework, including whether non-delivery penalties can offset moral-hazard incentives.

Climate risk and agricultural productivity

This research investigates how nonlinear temperature exposure and extreme heat affect crop productivity and how production practices can alter climate-related risk.

Agricultural policy and extension

I contribute to applied policy analysis related to U.S. farm programs, including ARC-CO and PLC, and translate economic research into extension and policy-oriented outputs.
